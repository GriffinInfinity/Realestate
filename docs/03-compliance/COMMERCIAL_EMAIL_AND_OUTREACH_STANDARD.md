# Commercial Email & Outreach Standard

## Purpose
Provide a controlled standard for legitimate business outreach used by the Realestate venture.

## Federal commercial-email baseline
The FTC states that CAN-SPAM applies to commercial email, including business-to-business email. Commercial messages must use accurate header information, avoid deceptive subject lines, identify the commercial nature of the message, include a valid physical postal address, provide a clear opt-out method, and honor opt-out requests within the required period. citehttps://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

This document is an operating standard, not legal advice. Applicable federal, state, local, platform, carrier, and industry requirements must be reviewed for the particular campaign and recipient population.

## Outreach eligibility gate
Before sending a commercial message:

- Recipient identity and business relevance are reasonably verified.
- The reason for contact is documented.
- The sender identity is accurate.
- The subject accurately describes the message.
- The message contains required commercial disclosures and a valid physical postal address.
- A working opt-out mechanism is available.
- Existing do-not-contact status has been checked.
- The contact is not being targeted through deceptive, harvested, or fabricated information.
- The message does not misrepresent licensing, representation, authority, property facts, or transaction status.

## Relationship-first standard
The objective is not maximum send volume. The objective is a legitimate business conversation with a relevant recipient.

Preferred sequence:

1. Research relevance.
2. Identify the specific business reason for contact.
3. Send a concise, truthful introduction.
4. Provide a useful reason to respond.
5. Record the communication.
6. If engaged, define the next action.
7. If no interest, stop or move to appropriate nurture status.
8. If the recipient opts out, record the restriction immediately and do not continue marketing outreach.

## Real-estate licensing boundary
Email outreach must not be used to perform or imply regulated brokerage activity before the required authorization exists.

TREC states that a sales agent must be sponsored by a licensed broker to perform acts of real-estate service, and an inactive sales-agent license does not authorize practice. TREC rules also state that listings and other agreements for brokerage services must be solicited and accepted in a broker's name, and compensation for a sponsored sales agent's regulated services must be handled through the sponsoring broker framework. citehttps://www.trec.texas.gov/node/122 citehttps://www.trec.texas.gov/agency-information/rules-and-laws/trec-rules

The system therefore separates:

- **Relationship development:** networking, introductions, business development, research, and other activity that does not constitute regulated brokerage activity.
- **Licensing-sensitive activity:** representation, negotiation, brokerage agreements, transaction services, or other activity that requires authorization.

A licensing-sensitive opportunity is routed to the compliance gate before action.

## 2026 Texas buyer workflow
TREC states that effective January 1, 2026, written agreements are required in specified residential buyer situations before showing residential property or presenting an offer on behalf of a prospective buyer. Non-representation showing arrangements have separate restrictions. citehttps://www.trec.texas.gov/article/what-changes-2026-about-buyer-tenant-representation-texas

Do not build an automated workflow that assumes a prospective residential buyer can simply be shown property or advised on a transaction without the applicable written agreement and broker-controlled process.

## Recordkeeping
For an active opportunity, preserve the substantive communication trail and the next action. TREC rules require brokers to maintain specified transaction records, including substantive communications and agreements, for at least four years under the stated rule framework. citehttps://www.trec.texas.gov/agency-information/rules-and-laws/trec-rules

## Automation rule
Automation may assist with organization, reminders, research, and administrative routing. It must not automatically perform a licensing-sensitive act merely because an opportunity reaches a pipeline stage.

## Definition of done
Every commercial outreach workflow has:

- Relevance verification
- Accurate sender identity
- Truthful subject and content
- Required commercial-email disclosures
- Physical postal address
- Opt-out mechanism
- Do-not-contact check
- Real-estate licensing gate
- Outlook classification
- Next-action discipline
- Audit trail
