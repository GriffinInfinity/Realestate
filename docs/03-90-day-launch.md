# First 90 Days

## Objective
Turn zero starting revenue into a measurable real-estate relationship and opportunity pipeline while simultaneously building the infrastructure required for licensed and investment operations.

## Days 1–14: Foundation

- Define brand, positioning, and service boundaries.
- Build the master prospect taxonomy.
- Build the partner taxonomy.
- Create Outlook categories for prospects, partners, active clients, opportunities, and compliance.
- Establish daily calendar operating blocks.
- Create standard email templates.
- Create a market research log.
- Identify Texas licensing path and education providers.
- Begin relationship outreach to licensed brokerages and real-estate professionals.

## Days 15–30: Pipeline creation

Target relationship categories:

- Independent brokerages
- Real-estate agents
- Property managers
- Lenders
- Title/escrow professionals
- Inspectors
- Contractors
- Investors
- Property owners
- Developers
- Commercial property professionals

Every prospect receives a record containing source, reason for contact, last contact, next action, and status.

## Days 31–60: Conversion system

- Measure reply rate.
- Measure qualified conversation rate.
- Measure appointment rate.
- Identify the highest-converting segment.
- Improve outreach based on observed results.
- Formalize partner onboarding.
- Build repeatable service packages.
- Develop licensing/sponsorship relationships.
- Build transaction-support SOPs.

## Days 61–90: Scale the winning motion

- Concentrate effort on proven segments.
- Expand the best-performing market cluster.
- Build referral loops.
- Document successful workflows.
- Train future operators through SOPs.
- Establish weekly KPI review.
- Establish monthly market review.
- Prepare the first revenue reinvestment budget.

## Daily scorecard

- New qualified prospects
- Personalized first contacts
- Follow-ups completed
- Replies
- Positive conversations
- Appointments
- Partner introductions
- Opportunities created
- Opportunities advanced
- Revenue
- Compliance exceptions

## Weekly decision rule

If a channel produces activity but no qualified conversations after a statistically meaningful sample, change the message, audience, or channel before increasing volume.

Never compensate for weak targeting with indiscriminate bulk outreach.
