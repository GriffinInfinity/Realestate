# Realestate Operating System

## Mission
Build a durable, globally scalable real-estate company over multiple years and market cycles, beginning with disciplined relationship and revenue development.

## Operating principles
- Protect licensing and regulatory boundaries before monetizing brokerage activity.
- Build relationships before fixed overhead.
- Record every qualified opportunity and its next action.
- Prefer repeatable processes over one-off wins.
- Reinvest operating cash flow into higher-value capabilities and assets.
- Expand geographically only after the operating model works locally.

## Phase 1 — Revenue & Relationship Engine
1. Build a qualified prospect universe.
2. Develop property-owner relationships.
3. Develop buyer and investor relationships.
4. Develop broker, lender, title, insurance, attorney, contractor, property-management, and other professional relationships.
5. Establish consistent outreach and follow-up standards.
6. Build market-intelligence routines.
7. Establish licensing and compliance gates.
8. Validate initial revenue models before adding significant fixed costs.

## Texas launch gate
Texas brokerage activity is regulated by TREC. A Texas sales agent must be sponsored by an active Texas broker to practice. Initial sales-agent education is 180 classroom hours, followed by the applicable examination, background-check, and sponsorship process. An inactive license does not authorize brokerage activity. See TREC's current requirements before performing any activity requiring licensure.

Source: https://www.trec.texas.gov/node/122

## 2026 compliance checkpoint
Texas changed buyer/tenant representation requirements effective January 1, 2026. Written agreements are required in specified residential buyer-representation circumstances, and non-representation showings have specific limitations. All client-facing processes must be reviewed against current TREC rules and applicable broker policies before deployment.

Source: https://www.trec.texas.gov/article/what-changes-2026-about-buyertenant-representation-texas

## Market intelligence baseline

Dallas was reported at a $425,000 median list price and 58 days on market in August 2026, while DFW active listings were down 4.4% year over year.

## CRM standard
Every active opportunity should contain:
- Contact/company
- Market/location
- Relationship type
- Opportunity type
- Source
- Qualification status
- Last contact date
- Next action
- Next-action date
- Estimated economic value
- Compliance/licensing flag
- Notes

## Weekly management cadence
Review:
- New qualified relationships
- Follow-ups due
- Active opportunities
- Revenue generated
- Pipeline value
- Partner development
- Licensing/compliance blockers
- Market changes
- Process improvements
- Highest-value actions for the next seven days

## Expansion architecture
Local relationship engine → Texas market coverage → licensed brokerage capability → transaction infrastructure → property-management/investment capabilities → owned assets → multi-market U.S. expansion → international partnerships and investment.

## Definition of progress
Progress is measured by qualified relationships, legitimate opportunities, closed revenue, operating cash flow, documented processes, compliance readiness, and durable assets—not by superficial activity volume.
