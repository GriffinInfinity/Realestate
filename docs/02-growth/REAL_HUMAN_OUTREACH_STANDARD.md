# Real-Human Outreach Standard

## Purpose

Kelvar LLC's outreach should sound like a real person starting a real business relationship. The goal is to open legitimate conversations, learn the market, and discover where Kelvar can create value.

## Voice

Write like Jameson is actually sending the message.

Use:
- Plain language.
- Short paragraphs.
- Specific reason for contacting the recipient.
- Honest description of Kelvar's current position.
- One or two useful questions.
- A simple next step.

Avoid:
- Corporate buzzwords.
- Artificially enthusiastic claims.
- "Revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "synergy", "transformative", or similar filler.
- Fake familiarity.
- Claims of expertise, clients, partnerships, licenses, transactions, or results that have not been established.
- Long service catalogs.
- Manufactured urgency.
- Pressure to schedule a call.

## Personalization

Every targeted message should answer:

1. Why this person or organization?
2. What did Kelvar actually notice?
3. Why is contact appropriate now?
4. What is Kelvar honestly trying to learn or accomplish?
5. What is the smallest reasonable next step?

Personalization must come from a reliable source. Do not infer personal interests or private information.

## Relationship-first positioning

Early outreach should generally introduce Kelvar and ask about:
- industry participation;
- appropriate associations or events;
- supplier/vendor pathways;
- professional relationships;
- referrals;
- education;
- community involvement;
- legitimate services where a concrete need is identified.

Do not pretend a prospect has a problem simply to create a sales pitch.

## Message length

Default target: 100–180 words.

Longer messages require a concrete reason.

## Human authenticity test

Before sending, ask:

> "Would this sound normal if Jameson had typed it himself after actually researching this person?"

If not, rewrite it.

## Compliance

Every commercial message must remain consistent with the Commercial Email and Outreach Standard.

Do not claim Kelvar is a licensed real-estate broker or agent unless the applicable license/sponsorship facts have been verified.

Where activity could constitute Texas-regulated brokerage activity, stop and apply the licensing/compliance gate.

Include truthful sender identification, required physical postal address and a working opt-out mechanism where required.

## Follow-up philosophy

Follow up because there is a legitimate reason:
- recipient replied;
- recipient requested information;
- a promised resource is ready;
- a relevant development occurred;
- a previously discussed next step is due.

Do not manufacture reasons to repeatedly contact someone.

## Measurement

Primary measures:
- meaningful conversations;
- introductions;
- referrals;
- qualified relationships;
- opportunities;
- realized revenue;
- response quality.

Secondary measures:
- response rate;
- time from first contact to conversation;
- follow-up completion;
- source-to-opportunity conversion.

Do not optimize for raw sends.

## Definition of done

A successful outreach system makes Kelvar recognizable as a genuine business, creates legitimate relationships, learns what the market actually needs, and converts only evidence-backed opportunities into the pipeline.
