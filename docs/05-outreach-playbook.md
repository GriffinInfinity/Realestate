# Relationship Outreach Playbook

## Purpose
Create legitimate business relationships without deceptive, indiscriminate, or high-volume spam behavior.

## Priority sequence

1. Warm relationships already available to the business.
2. Professional real-estate partners.
3. Local property and investment professionals.
4. Business owners with real-estate needs.
5. Property owners where there is a legitimate, specific reason to contact them.
6. Broader market development only after the earlier layers are working.

## Research standard

Before contacting a prospect, establish:

- Who they are
- What they do
- What market they serve
- Why the relationship could be useful
- What specific reason makes the outreach relevant

Do not fabricate familiarity, claims, property information, credentials, or prior interactions.

## Email structure

1. Relevant subject.
2. Short introduction.
3. Specific reason for contact.
4. Clear value proposition.
5. Simple call to action.
6. Professional signature.

## Follow-up sequence

- Day 0: initial relevant message.
- Day 3–5: useful follow-up.
- Day 10–14: final short follow-up.
- Then move to nurture unless the person requests otherwise.

Adjust timing for replies, holidays, market events, or explicit preferences.

## Stop conditions

Immediately stop outreach when:

- Recipient opts out.
- Address is invalid.
- Recipient asks not to be contacted.
- Contact is clearly irrelevant.
- The business cannot substantiate the claim being made.

## Quality standard

A smaller number of highly relevant conversations is preferable to a larger number of low-quality contacts.

## Real-estate-specific rule

Do not describe the company as a licensed broker/agent or offer regulated brokerage services until the required licensing, sponsorship, and advertising controls are verified for the applicable jurisdiction.
