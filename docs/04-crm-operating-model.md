# CRM Operating Model

## Contact classes

### Prospect
Potential client, owner, investor, agent, broker, vendor, or strategic partner.

### Qualified relationship
A prospect with a verified reason for engagement and a realistic business need.

### Opportunity
A qualified relationship with a defined next action and potential economic value.

### Client
A party currently receiving an agreed service within the company's lawful scope.

### Strategic partner
A professional or organization capable of repeatedly creating value for the network.

## Required fields

- Name
- Organization
- Role
- Market
- Contact information
- Source
- Relationship class
- Primary need
- Last contact date
- Next action
- Next-action date
- Status
- Opportunity value, when reasonably known
- Licensing/compliance classification
- Notes

## Pipeline states

`RESEARCH → CONTACTED → REPLIED → QUALIFIED → APPOINTMENT → OPPORTUNITY → ACTIVE → CLOSED/WON → NURTURE`

Use `CLOSED/NO FIT` when the relationship is not currently appropriate.

## Follow-up discipline

Every active opportunity must have a next action. No opportunity should remain in an active state without an owner and next-action date.

## Relationship principle

The goal is not maximum contact volume. The goal is maximum useful relationships per unit of time.

## Outlook implementation

Use Outlook categories to make the mailbox function as the lightweight operating CRM during the zero-capital phase. Calendar appointments become the authoritative scheduling layer. GitHub documentation remains the authoritative process layer.
