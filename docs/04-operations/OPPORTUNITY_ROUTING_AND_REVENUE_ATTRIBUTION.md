# Opportunity Routing & Revenue Attribution Layer

## Purpose
Provide a controlled path from a verified relationship or market signal to a legitimate opportunity, while keeping estimated pipeline economics separate from realized revenue.

## Opportunity intake
Every candidate opportunity must capture:
- Opportunity ID
- Originating Record ID
- Origin type: Relationship, Referral, Introduction, Market Signal, Inbound Request, Other
- Property/project and market when known
- Business need or requested service
- Decision-maker status
- Evidence/source
- Evidence date
- Licensing/compliance classification
- Opportunity stage
- Next action and date
- Estimated value and estimate basis
- Actual revenue and revenue date when realized

## Routing stages
1. **Intake** — candidate signal captured; no assumption of commercial viability.
2. **Verify** — identity, project, need, authority and source are checked.
3. **Compliance Review** — regulated activity, licensing, representation and commercial terms are classified.
4. **Qualified Opportunity** — sufficient evidence exists to justify active business development.
5. **Active** — an agreed or clearly documented business process is underway.
6. **Won** — revenue or another defined commercial outcome is actually realized.
7. **Nurture** — legitimate interest exists but timing or readiness is not current.
8. **Disqualified** — evidence, fit, authority, compliance or legitimacy does not support advancement.

## Routing rules
- No opportunity advances solely because an email was opened or answered.
- A positive conversation is not the same as a qualified opportunity.
- A referral is not revenue until a real commercial outcome occurs.
- Licensing-sensitive activity pauses at Compliance Review until the required authorization is established.
- Unknown decision-maker status remains unknown; do not infer authority.
- Financial estimates must show their basis and must never be reported as realized revenue.

## Revenue attribution
When revenue is realized, record:
- Revenue ID
- Opportunity ID
- Customer/counterparty
- Service or transaction category
- Gross amount received/recognized
- Revenue date
- Attribution source
- Any applicable costs or splits when known
- Supporting record/reference

Revenue attribution should answer:
1. What generated the opportunity?
2. Which relationship or market signal created the path?
3. What service or commercial activity produced the revenue?
4. When was revenue actually realized?
5. Which assumptions were estimates rather than facts?

## Relationship credit
Multiple relationships may contribute to one opportunity. Record contribution factually rather than assigning subjective credit percentages unless a documented agreement establishes them.

## Management metrics
Primary:
- Qualified opportunities
- Active opportunities
- Won opportunities
- Realized revenue
- Time from qualified relationship to opportunity
- Time from opportunity to realized revenue

Diagnostic:
- Source-to-opportunity conversion
- Relationship-to-introduction conversion
- Introduction-to-qualified conversion
- Opportunity aging
- Stalled opportunity count
- Compliance-review cycle time
- Revenue by verified source
- Revenue by service category

## Controls
- DNC/opt-out status overrides commercial outreach.
- Preserve source evidence and dates.
- Never fabricate revenue, deal status, customer relationships or authority.
- Never use estimated pipeline as a claim of business performance.
- Material contractual, financial, brokerage or regulated actions require the appropriate human authorization.

## Definition of done
An opportunity is production-ready when its origin, evidence, business need, responsible relationship, compliance classification, stage, economics and next action can be understood by another operator without relying on undocumented assumptions.
