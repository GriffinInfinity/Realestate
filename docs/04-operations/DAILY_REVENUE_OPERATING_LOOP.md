# Daily Revenue Operating Loop

## Objective
Create a disciplined daily system that converts market information and legitimate relationships into qualified opportunities, scheduled follow-ups, partner relationships, and eventually revenue.

## Daily sequence

### 08:30 — Pipeline build
- Review new prospects.
- Review market intelligence.
- Review responses and follow-ups due.
- Identify opportunities requiring action.
- Check compliance flags.

### 09:00 — Research and qualification
For each new prospect:
1. Verify the source.
2. Verify the business relevance.
3. Identify the relationship type.
4. Identify the reason for contact.
5. Determine whether outreach is appropriate.
6. Record the next action.

### Outreach window
Use targeted communication only when there is a documented business reason.

Every commercial email must satisfy the applicable compliance checklist before sending. CAN-SPAM requires accurate headers, non-deceptive subject lines, identification of commercial messages where applicable, a valid physical postal address, and a clear opt-out method. Opt-outs must be honored within the statutory period. Source: FTC CAN-SPAM Compliance Guide.

### Response processing
For every substantive response:
- Categorize the relationship.
- Record the business need.
- Record the last contact.
- Assign one next action.
- Assign a date for that action.
- Escalate licensing-sensitive activity before proceeding.

### Opportunity review
An opportunity is not considered active unless:
- A real business need exists.
- The relevant decision-maker or responsible party is identified where reasonably possible.
- Timing is understood or being qualified.
- The next action is explicit.
- Any licensing/compliance gate is identified.

## Texas brokerage gate
Texas TREC states that a sales agent must be sponsored by a licensed broker to perform real-estate services. An inactive sales-agent license does not authorize brokerage activity. Source: TREC, Become a Real Estate Sales Agent.

Before any activity that may constitute brokerage activity:
- Identify the activity.
- Determine whether a license is required.
- Verify active status and sponsorship where applicable.
- Follow the sponsoring broker's policies.
- Do not represent, negotiate, advertise brokerage services, or accept transaction compensation outside the applicable framework.

TREC also states that listings and other agreements for brokerage services must be solicited and accepted in a broker's name, and that sponsoring brokers retain responsibility for sponsored sales-agent activities and advertising compliance.

## End-of-day control
Before ending the day:
- No qualified prospect is left without a next action.
- Follow-up-due items are scheduled or resolved.
- Do-not-contact requests are recorded.
- Opportunities have current status.
- Revenue events are recorded.
- Compliance blockers are visible.
- Tomorrow's highest-value actions are identified.

## Weekly management metrics
Track:
- New qualified prospects
- Meaningful conversations
- Follow-ups completed
- Opportunities created
- Opportunities advanced
- Revenue closed
- Pipeline value
- Partner relationships created
- Response rate
- Time from first contact to qualified opportunity
- Compliance escalations
- Nurture conversions

## Quality controls
Never optimize the system solely for:
- Number of emails sent
- Number of contacts added
- Number of raw prospects collected
- Superficial activity volume

Optimize for:
- Legitimate relationships
- Qualified needs
- Appropriate conversations
- Repeatable processes
- Authorized revenue
- Long-term relationship value

## Definition of done
The daily loop is operational when the business can begin each day with a known pipeline, process new prospects consistently, conduct compliant outreach, respond to conversations, advance legitimate opportunities, apply licensing gates, and finish with a measurable next-action queue for the following day.
