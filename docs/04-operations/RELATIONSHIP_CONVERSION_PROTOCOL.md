# Relationship Conversion Protocol

## Objective
Convert verified outreach into genuine business relationships and opportunities without optimizing for raw email volume.

## 1. Response Triage
Every inbound response is assigned one of:
- Conversation — genuine response with no immediate opportunity.
- Introduction — recipient offers another person/contact.
- Opportunity — specific project, property, service need, or commercial discussion emerges.
- Nurture — positive but timing is unclear.
- DNC/Opt-Out — suppress future commercial outreach immediately.
- Needs Review — ambiguous, legal, licensing, financial, or authority issue.

## 2. Response Rules
- Reply personally and address the actual question first.
- Never claim a license, representation authority, client relationship, project, transaction, or capability that has not been established.
- Do not pressure a contact into a meeting.
- Preserve the person's preferred communication channel when known.
- Record the source message, date, relationship stage, and exactly one next action.
- A referral is recorded separately from the originating relationship.

## 3. Opportunity Gate
An opportunity cannot advance solely because someone responded positively. Record:
1. What is actually being discussed.
2. Who the decision-maker is.
3. Property/project and market, if applicable.
4. Service requested or potential revenue path.
5. Licensing/compliance classification.
6. Commercial terms only after appropriate authority and review.
7. One dated next action.

## 4. Introduction Gate
When someone introduces Kelvar to another person:
- Thank the introducer.
- Record both relationships.
- Verify the introduced person's identity/role before treating them as qualified.
- Reference the introduction truthfully.
- Do not imply endorsement beyond what was actually said.

## 5. Follow-Up Discipline
- No automatic follow-up merely because a message was sent.
- Follow up only when there is a legitimate business reason.
- Respect opt-outs immediately.
- If a conversation has gone quiet, use a useful, low-pressure reason to reconnect.
- Close or nurture stale records rather than repeatedly contacting them.

## 6. Metrics
Primary:
- Meaningful conversations
- Introductions
- Qualified relationships
- Opportunities
- Realized revenue

Secondary:
- Response rate
- Time to conversation
- Follow-up completion
- Source-to-opportunity conversion
- Opportunity-to-revenue conversion
- Opt-out rate

## 7. Current State — 2026-09-18
The current campaign has produced a substantial verified outreach base across associations, developers, land development, property management, title/transaction infrastructure and commercial real estate leadership. No substantive new inbound response was identified in the latest mailbox check. The next operating priority is therefore controlled monitoring and conversion rather than indiscriminate volume expansion.
