# Pipeline Stage Definition

## Purpose
Give every relationship and opportunity a consistent state so the business can measure movement without confusing activity with progress.

| Stage | Entry condition | Exit condition | Required fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| New | Source identified | Relevance verified | source, relationship type |
| Qualified | Legitimate business relevance established | Appropriate outreach/contact occurs | reason for contact, source |
| Engaged | Two-way interaction exists | Specific need or next step identified | last contact, notes, next action |
| Opportunity | Specific business need exists | Authorized work begins or relationship moves to nurture | need, timing, value estimate, compliance flag |
| Active | Opportunity is progressing through an authorized process | Outcome reached | next action, compliance status |
| Won | Revenue-producing outcome completed | Closed | revenue, date, source |
| Nurture | Legitimate relationship without immediate active need | New need emerges or relationship closes | nurture reason, review date |
| Disqualified | Not suitable or not legitimate | Closed | disqualification reason |

## Stage-control rules

1. Never skip qualification merely to inflate the pipeline.
2. Every active record has one next action.
3. Every next action has a date.
4. Every opportunity has a compliance/licensing classification.
5. A compliance blocker pauses advancement rather than being hidden in notes.
6. A do-not-contact request overrides normal outreach cadence.
7. Closed records retain enough information to understand the outcome and source.

## Opportunity value discipline

Estimated value is a planning field, not a promise. Record the basis for an estimate and update it when better information becomes available.

Do not count speculative future revenue as closed revenue.

## Conversation discipline

A conversation becomes commercially meaningful when the other party identifies a real need, timing, property, portfolio, referral, service requirement, or other actionable business context.

## Compliance discipline

If an activity may require a Texas real-estate license, stop stage advancement at the compliance gate until the required authorization is verified. TREC states that sales agents must be sponsored by an active licensed broker to perform brokerage services and that an inactive sales agent may not practice.

## Management view

The weekly review should be able to answer:
- How many qualified relationships were created?
- How many became meaningful conversations?
- How many became legitimate opportunities?
- How many opportunities advanced?
- What revenue actually closed?
- Which opportunities are blocked?
- Which relationships need follow-up?
- Where did the strongest opportunities originate?

## Definition of done
A manager can inspect any pipeline record and understand its current state, why it is there, what happened last, what happens next, whether a compliance gate exists, and whether the reported economics are realized or estimated.
