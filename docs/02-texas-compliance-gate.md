# Texas Compliance Gate

## Purpose
Prevent the business from accidentally performing regulated real-estate activity before the required licensing and broker relationships are in place.

## Verified TREC facts

- A Texas sales agent is licensed to act on behalf of a real-estate broker and must be sponsored by a licensed broker to perform real-estate services.
- TREC states that an inactive sales-agent license cannot be used to practice until the agent is sponsored by an active Texas broker.
- TREC rules require listings and other agreements for real-estate brokerage services to be solicited and accepted in a broker's name.
- TREC rules govern advertising and require broker oversight for sponsored sales agents.
- Compensation to a sales agent for brokerage activity must flow through or have the written consent of the sponsoring broker.
- Texas licensing rules can apply to brokerage business conducted from Texas even when the property is elsewhere.

## Launch posture before licensing

Permitted operating focus:

- Market research
- Public business research
- Relationship development
- General educational content that does not hold the business out as a licensed agent/broker
- Administrative and technology services that are independently lawful
- Scheduling and communications
- Partner identification
- B2B operational support
- Internal market analysis

Do not:

- Hold the company out as a Texas real-estate broker or sales agent without the required authority.
- Negotiate a real-estate transaction on behalf of another party when the activity requires a license.
- Solicit or accept brokerage agreements as though the company were licensed.
- Quote or accept brokerage commissions outside the legally authorized structure.
- Advertise licensed brokerage services without the required broker relationship and disclosures.

## Licensing path

1. Confirm the desired Texas license level.
2. Complete required education.
3. Complete application/background requirements.
4. Pass required examinations.
5. Establish sponsorship with an active broker for sales-agent practice.
6. Establish compliant advertising and document-retention procedures.
7. Build supervised transaction experience.
8. Later evaluate broker-level requirements and whether vertical integration is economically justified.

TREC's current sales-agent page states that the initial education requirement is 180 classroom hours and that an applicant receives an inactive license until sponsorship is established. TREC's rules also describe additional education and broker-license requirements.

## Compliance operating rule

Every new service or revenue stream gets classified as:

- `GREEN` — clearly outside regulated brokerage activity.
- `YELLOW` — legal review required before launch.
- `RED` — do not perform until the required license/authority is verified.

No revenue target overrides this gate.
