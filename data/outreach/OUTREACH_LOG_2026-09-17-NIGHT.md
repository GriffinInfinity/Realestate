## Zero-budget operating principle
Until Kelvar has capital available for paid memberships, sponsorships, advertising or property acquisition:
- prioritize free introductions;
- use public events/resources where attendance is free or inexpensive;
- build relationships before paying for memberships;
- learn licensing requirements before performing regulated activity;
- pursue service, referral, vendor and partnership opportunities that do not require capital;
- separate learning/networking from regulated brokerage activity.

## Important distinction
Getting started in real estate does not require immediately buying property. The initial objective is to build knowledge, relationships, credibility, a compliant operating structure and eventually a revenue-producing service or transaction pathway.
