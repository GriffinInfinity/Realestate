## Contact-confidence standard

A contact is not “verified” merely because a person or email appears in a search result.

- **C0:** no contact identified.
- **C1:** organization-level official contact only.
- **C2:** named role/person confirmed by an official organization/project source.
- **C3:** direct business email/phone confirmed by an authoritative source and role matches the intended relationship.
- **C4:** contact validated through a current business interaction or authoritative response.

Only C2+ may be considered for personalized relationship outreach. C3+ is preferred. Search-result-only emails remain unverified.

## Qualification gate

Do not advance a candidate to **Qualified** unless:
1. Identity and role are sufficiently verified.
2. Source and research date are preserved.
3. A specific business reason exists.
4. Communication is appropriate and compliant.
5. DNC/opt-out status is clear.
6. Texas licensing implications are classified.
7. One dated next action exists.

## Operating rule

The objective is **verified relationships and legitimate conversations**, not maximum prospect count or email volume.

No record becomes an outreach target solely because a property is being developed, a government incentive exists, financing was approved, a company appears in a public agenda, or a generic email address can be found.

Every relationship must earn advancement through evidence and a concrete business reason.
