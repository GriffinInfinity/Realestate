## Contact-confidence standard
- C0: no contact identified.
- C1: organization-level official contact only.
- C2: named role/person confirmed by an official organization/project source.
- C3: direct business email/phone confirmed by an authoritative source and role matches intended relationship.
- C4: contact validated through current business interaction or authoritative response.

Only C2+ may be considered for personalized relationship outreach. C3+ is preferred. Search-result-only emails remain unverified.

## Qualification gate
Do not advance to Qualified unless identity/role, source/date, specific business reason, communication readiness, DNC/opt-out status, licensing classification, and a dated next action are established.
