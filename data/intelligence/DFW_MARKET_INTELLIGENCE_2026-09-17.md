## Operating rules
- Do not convert a market signal into a qualified prospect without identity, role, source, research date, business reason, communication-readiness, DNC/opt-out and compliance checks.
- Preserve the distinction between public-program intelligence and evidence about a specific organization.
- Do not fabricate contact details or property facts.
- Estimated opportunity value is not revenue.
- Any activity that may constitute Texas-regulated brokerage activity stays behind the licensing/compliance gate.
- Every qualified/engaged/opportunity/active relationship must have one dated next action.
