# Realestate

## Mission
Build a durable, technology-enabled real-estate company that can expand from a zero-capital launch into a multi-market, eventually global real-estate platform.

The company will earn trust first, build transaction capability legally, compound relationships, reinvest operating cash flow, and expand market-by-market.

## Operating doctrine

1. Start asset-light.
2. Generate revenue before taking unnecessary fixed costs.
3. Build a real pipeline every day.
4. Treat every contact as a relationship, not a number.
5. Never represent licensing authority that the business does not currently possess.
6. Separate lead generation, education, brokerage, investing, property management, and development into clearly defined business lines.
7. Document processes before scaling them.
8. Reinvest cash flow into people, technology, marketing, data, and eventually property.
9. Build for decades, not quarters.

## Current market context — September 2026

NAR reported August 2026 existing-home sales at a 3.98 million seasonally adjusted annual rate, with 1.62 million units of unsold inventory and 4.9 months of supply. Median existing-home price was $429,100, up 1.6% year over year. This supports an operating model centered on disciplined lead generation, inventory intelligence, and relationship-driven conversion rather than assuming a uniformly fast market.

Realtor.com reports that active listings continue to recover nationally while remaining below pre-pandemic levels. Its 2026 forecast describes a more balanced national market and meaningful variation among metro areas. Market selection will therefore be data-driven rather than based on a single national narrative.

## Texas launch constraint

Texas brokerage activity is regulated by the Texas Real Estate Commission. A Texas sales agent must be sponsored by an active broker to practice. Brokerage advertising and compensation are also subject to TREC rules. Until the required licensing/sponsorship status is established, this project will not represent itself as a licensed brokerage or perform activities requiring a license.
